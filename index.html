Motivation

ORES ranked edits for patrollers, with three gaps

Volunteers, the patrollers, check new edits by hand. In 2022, Wikimedia's ORES sorted their queue.

ORES, the baseline

  • 28 languages, a separate model for each
  • Reads text as lists of bad words
  • Flags anonymous editors more

This paper

  • One model for 47 languages
  • Multilingual BERT reads the change
  • Reports its bias against anonymous edits

Can one model rank better than ORES and be fairer to anonymous editors?

Task and labels

Task: will this edit be undone?

An edit undone by a later one counts as reverted. Reverts are free labels, but edit wars make them noisy:

Rule in the authors' code: drop an edit if the edit that reverted it was itself reverted.
Bots, page creations, talk and user pages are left out too.

Fairness · what to aim for

What should the gap be?

Two targets you can defend. The paper picks 7.93, in a footnote: “the closer to 7.93, the better.”

Reverts are patrollers' decisions: mirror them, and any bias in them becomes the target.
